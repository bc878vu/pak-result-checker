Privacy Policy

Pak Result Checker is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. Search requests are sent only to the result service configured for the selected board when a live authorized provider is available.

Result searches

We do not claim a result is official unless it is returned by an authorized result source. Boards that require CAPTCHA, session verification or other protected access are linked to their official result service instead of being bypassed.

Advertising and cookies

The website may use Google AdSense or another approved advertising provider after approval and configuration. Advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertising according to their policies and the consent choices available in the visitor's region.

We do not sell student search information to advertisers. Do not enter unnecessary personal information into the checker.

Third-party links

Board websites, result portals and document resources are operated by their respective owners. Their privacy practices are separate from this website.

Security

Provider credentials are intended to remain server-side and are not published in the website interface or source repository.

Contact

For privacy questions or corrections, use the site's Contact page.
