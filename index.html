IMPORTANT

Why can some boards open an official portal?

Some official portals use CAPTCHA, session tokens, anti-bot checks or other board-specific verification. Pak Result Checker never bypasses those protections.

When an authorized direct provider is unavailable, the selected board remains accessible through its official result service instead of showing invented or unverified marks.
